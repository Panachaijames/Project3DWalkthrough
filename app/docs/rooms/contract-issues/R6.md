## 2. Items that need a decision

### D-1 PlanCanvasProps has no place for the fixes that wait for a Rebuild (R-6.4 "Shown before Rebuild")

R-6.4 draws them on the plan (a line dashed orange, the room outlined, a marker, an arrow at the picked bottom, the
removed closing struck through, the area hatched grey). The frozen props carry the overlay, flags, selection, set-asides
and diff only. Worked around in R6's files: the Check step hands the canvas a shallow copy of the floor's overlay made by
`plan/marks.ts` `withMarks(overlay, marks)`, and the canvas reads the marks with `marksOf(overlay)` (a WeakMap keyed by
that copy; memoised, so the canvas rebuilds its local overlay only when the overlay or the fixes change). **Proposed:**
accept as built; or RI adds an optional `marks?: FixMarks` to `PlanCanvasProps` (one line in R0's `index.ts`) and the
Check step passes it instead (two lines).

### D-2 "The hint shown while a line is being drawn (after the first click)" (`TID.drawLineHint`)

The page cannot know of the first click: the frozen callback `onLine(a, b)` reports both ends at the second click. Built:
the hint (`TID.drawLineHint`, "Draw a line: click its two ends (each snaps to a drawn corner within 6 px); Esc drops the
first end.") shows while the tool Draw a line is chosen, and the canvas itself marks the first end (a cross) and draws a
dashed line from it to the pointer until the second click. **Proposed:** the test id's comment reads "while the tool Draw
a line is on"; or an optional `onLineStart?(a)` callback in R0's `index.ts`.

### D-3 The provisional house-rooms overlay writes the upper floor's holes and stairs in the build frame (for RI-0)

`fixtures/upload/overlay/house-rooms.overlay.json` (the tracer's, R0.md 4) gives the upper floor's spaces in its sheet
frame (x 82 - 124 m) but its two holes and two stairs 74.022 m off (x 22 - 42 m, the build frame: R1.md N-11). Drawn as
they are they fall outside the floor's sheet. `plan-canvas.spec.ts` therefore probes the house's holes only where they lie
inside the floor's area (none with this fixture; the test says so in a comment) and probes the hatched holes on the
reference building's upper floor (its stair well and void, always). **For RI-0:** the replacement overlay must have every
field in the floor's sheet metres; the house's hole probe then runs by itself. No change to the canvas.

### Decided by the main session (11 Nov 2026, between batches)

* **D-1: accepted as built.** The fixes waiting for a Rebuild ride on the overlay copy through the WeakMap in
  `plan/marks.ts`; the frozen `PlanCanvasProps` stays as it is.
* **D-2: accepted, for RI.** The hint shows while the tool is on and the canvas marks the first end; RI rewords the
  comment of `TID.drawLineHint` to say so (a comment only; no new callback).
* **D-3: for RI-0.** RI replaces the provisional house-rooms overlay with one made by a real new-rule build, in sheet
  metres, and checks that the house hole probe of `plan-canvas.spec.ts` then finds the holes.

## 3. How R6 read the design where it was silent or loose (information; accepted unless RI says otherwise)

* **N-1 Which way a draft is built** is read from `options.slabs` only: `'network'` is "the new way" (a legal mix such as
  network with ceilings off is still new); anything else, a missing key included, "the earlier way" (R-1.2). The button
  and the fold's first control write all three options (`NEW_RULES` / `OLD_RULES`); the second control writes `ceilings`
  alone and is shown only with the new way.
* **N-2 The checklist's rules line** is a warning (never blocks) naming the Floors step, last in the list; its `<li>`
  carries `TID.checklistRules` instead of `checklist-item`, so the existing "first warning" selectors keep meaning what
  they meant.
* **N-3 A room fix's point.** `onSpace(id)` gives only the space id (frozen), so the fix's `at` is a point well inside the
  room's outline (`plan/geom.ts` `insidePoint`: of the middles of the inside spans of 30 scan lines, the one farthest from
  every edge), kept to the millimetre; the room panel shows it in `data-at`. A place with no room (`onPoint`) keeps the
  clicked point. "It is outdoors" on a `slab-gap` flag writes a point inside the unfloored area whose mouth is the
  flag's (the flag's `at` is the middle of the mouth, on the area's edge), else the flag's point.
* **N-4 One fix per room or stair.** The panel's fix of a room is the room fix whose point lies inside that room; of a
  place with no room, the one within 0.5 m or in the same unfloored area; of a stair, the stair fix that `stairHolding`
  (outline holds it, or within 0.5 m) gives that stair. Every change replaces that fix by its point (never by a list
  index, so a 412 re-applies it on the right record); a fix that asks for nothing (no name, auto, auto / no bottom, auto)
  is taken out ("set back", "Decide automatically").
* **N-5 "Waiting for Rebuild"** is shown for a fix while the build is stale (`result.stale`) and the last overlay does not
  show it applied: a closing line has a staff closing with its index whose ends lie within 0.6 m of the fix's (the builder
  extends each end by up to 0.5 m); a removed closing has no app closing left within 0.3 m of its ends; a room fix's name
  is the name of the room holding its point, "no ceiling" makes that room's `ceiling` false, "no floor here" / "it is
  outdoors" is a hole or an answered area there, "floor here" is plate with no open area there; a stair fix's stair has
  the asked state, and climbs from the picked end (its bottom nearer the pick than its top). A build that is not stale
  shows nothing waiting (an unmatched fix is the builder's `fix-unmatched` flag).
* **N-6 A click on one of the app's closings** with Rooms and stairs takes it out (`use: 'none'` with the overlay's two
  ends), and a second click on it puts it back (removes that fix). No panel: testids.ts has none for a closing, and R-6.2
  writes the fix from the click.
* **N-7 Draw a line snaps to the nearest vertex of any drawn item within 6 px.** `pick-point` (U7's, unchanged) snaps to a
  vertex of the nearest item only; on a plan a wall corner is often nearer another item's edge than its own vertices, and
  the first click then did not snap at all (`canvas.test.tsx` found it on the reference plan). A click with no vertex
  within 6 px keeps its own point. A line of no length reports nothing.
* **N-8 The 'rooms' tool** sends a click to a stair whose outline holds the point or whose outline or walking line is
  within 6 px, then to an app closing (bridge, doorway; never a staff line) within 6 px, then to the room holding it
  (outline minus holes), else to `onPoint`. Flags and openings are not clicked with it (the Select tool still does).
* **N-9 What the ticks switch.** Rooms: the rooms' tint and outline, and their line in the hover text. Floor plate: the
  slab outlines, the holes' hatch, the unfloored areas' hatch and mouths. Furniture: the overlay's furniture boxes (the
  drawing's own furniture lines stay: they are the drawing). Stairs, closings, openings, parts, flags and the waiting
  marks are always drawn. `ceiling` parts are never drawn on the plan (R-5.3); treads and landings are drawn as parts.
* **N-10 The Build and Save steps' counts** are shown only when the overlay has `spaces` (a build with the new rules):
  rooms closed = `spaces.length`, names placed = `rooms.length` (the overlay's labels), stairs you can climb = stairs with
  `state: 'climb'` (R5.md D-3).
* **N-11 Existing tests changed on purpose**: `apps/web/test/build/logic.test.ts` ("clear for the bank fixture" now
  expects the rules line for the fixture, which has none of the three keys, and nothing with the new rules; the
  conversion-loss case uses the new rules) and `furniture-words.test.ts` (the checklist's items without the rules line).
  `apps/web/test/plan/kit.ts`'s recording context gained `clip` (the hatches clip).
* **N-12 The set-aside tick** "The floor runs past it" is shown only while the draft is built the new way (it means
  nothing to the old floor plates); `floorPast` is written only when ticked.
* **N-13 The Heights panel** shows the ceiling height of the floor on screen (one input, `data-key`), as it shows that
  floor's level and wall height; the Floors step shows every floor's.
* **N-14 A lost floor** (`plate area (m2)` smaller by 2 m2 or more) is listed first whatever floor is on screen; only the
  first such row carries `TID.lostFloor` (one element per id). The rows keep the raw key in `data-what`; the screen shows
  "Rooms closed", "Floor plate (m²)" ("Floor plate (m²): floor lost" on the lost row) and "Stairs you can climb". R3.md
  D-3 (RI: holes cut on purpose are no lost floor) changes the builder only.
* **N-15 A room name** is typed in a one-line box of at most 200 characters; control characters become spaces and blank is
  no name (`cleanRoomName`, the API's rule of R5.md N-3). Markup is text: the name goes to React text only.
* **N-16 The mock API** never builds: its "rebuild" of the reference building applies the fixes the simple way (a staff
  line drawn, an app closing gone, an area answered or floored, a room named / ceiling-less / a "no floor" hole, a stair
  kept as lines or climbing from its picked bottom; their flags go). Two rooms never become one there.
