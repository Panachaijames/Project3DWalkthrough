## 1. `treads` in the stairway record (R-8.5 against R-8.1)

R-8.5's example has `risers: 21, treads: 20, landings: 1`. By R-8.1 tread j lies between riser j and riser j + 1, and a
landing after riser k takes the place between riser k and riser k + 1: a stair of 21 risers with one landing has 19
tread parts and 1 landing part. **The fixtures count parts**: `treads` = the stairway's `tread` parts, `landings` = its
`landing` parts (the reference building: 16 risers, 14 treads, 1 landing; a straight flight of n risers: n - 1 treads).
The tracer does the same. R2 follows the fixtures; RI: accept, or change the fixture maker and the one line of R-8.5.

## 2. The door finder finds clean gaps as passages (R-2.5, R-2.6 against the frozen detector)

The reference building is meant to have "a 2.9 m opening with no door in an outside wall" (an unfloored area with a
mouth) and "a door-sized gap between two wall ends" (a doorway closing). Drawn the plain way (two ends of one 0.2 m wall
line facing each other), the frozen door finder reports both as `passage` openings (`vendor/openings.py` `passage_ok`:
both ends wall ends of one wall line, up to 3.6 m, in walls of 7 cm or more). Their spans then close the rooms (R-2.1
point 2), today's plate floors the garage, and neither case exists.

So the reference building draws them the way the detector does not take: the wide opening runs from a wall's END to the
SIDE of another wall (an end-to-face gap is a passage only up to 1.6 m), and the door-sized gap stands in a 0.06 m
partition (thinner than the detector's 7 cm; R-2.6's wall end is 0.04 - 0.5 m). The detector then finds the three drawn
doors and nothing else, on both floors. **For R1**: a synthetic test of R-2.6 or of the unfloored rules needs the same
care, or the fake detector (`test_u3_helpers.fake_detector`). On real drawings the missed doorways of RA-18 are made by
taking a found door out, which is unaffected.

## 3. The rows of the new report keys are the fixtures' (R-12.3 types them only as lists)

`contracts.py` types `rooms.open`, `rooms.several`, `slab.holes`, `slab.unfloored`, `slab.closings`, `slab.dropped`,
`stairways`, `stairClash` and the three `fixes` lists as `list`. R1 (who writes them) and R3 (who reads them) do not
talk, so their rows are fixed by `fixtures/rooms/build-result.reference.json` and listed in `fixtures/README.md`. Two
rows carry more than the design names, because the overlay needs it: the model's `voids` are a point and an area, so
`slab.holes` rows carry the hole's outline `o` (for `OverlayFloor.holes`), with `cut: true`, or `cut: false` and `why`
for a mark that was not cut (the `hole-skipped` flag); `slab.unfloored` rows carry `o` and `mouth` (for
`OverlayFloor.unfloored` and the `slab-gap` flag's `a`, `b`). `StairsResult.pairs` has no place in the report: a
`stairs-none` flag can be told from the floors' `stairways` rows (no row with `state: 'climb'` and `to` = the next
floor), so none was added.

## 4. What the fixtures are, and what they are not

* **Computed by their own script, through the builder's wiring.** `tools/rooms/make-reference-building.py` reads the
  plans with the app's reader and runs `build()` with the five calls of the rules answered from the building's own
  definition, the stairs by R-8.1, R-8.2, R-8.3 and R-8.5 as written. The walls, openings, lintels, collider skins, the
  model frame, `clean()` and the replay come from the unchanged code and from R0's wiring in `build/bank.py`
  (`build_floors_network`, `apply_cuts`) and `build/model.py`. **`--check` (the same bytes again) holds at R0's state
  only**: once R1 rewrites that wiring the tool is the record of how the fixtures were made, not a way to re-make them.
  The fixtures do not change.
* **The stairway's `walk`** is: 1.0 m before riser 1, the middle of the first and of the last riser of each flight, the
  middle of each landing, 1.0 m past riser n. R-8.5 says "through the middle of each flight and landing": R2's contract
  test should compare the line, not the list of points, if its reader keeps more points.
* **The ground floor's hall is closed by the doorway closing only**, its room outline is the analytic rectangle (the
  0.1 m cupboard wall inside it is not cut out of the room).
* **No name on the terrace.** By R-3.2 a name on a terrace belongs to no room and would be flagged `room-open` ("its
  room is not closed"), which is wrong for a terrace. The design is silent; the building has no such text. RI: decide
  whether a name on a terrace is flagged.
* **The overlay fixture's new fields and flags are written by the fixture maker** from the report, in the shapes of
  R-12.4 and with the texts of R-6.1 (`title` is a short phrase of the scaffold's, `detail` the design's sentence).
  `out/overlay.py` does not write them yet (R3). `fixtures/rooms/flags.every-kind.json` adds a hand-written
  `stairs-none`, which a building whose stair climbs cannot raise.
* **`fixtures/upload/overlay/house-rooms.overlay.json` is provisional and is the TRACER's**, not "from the trial" as
  ACCEPTANCE 2.5 says (the trial's scratch output is not in the project): the house built with `house-rooms.json` by
  the tracer, the new fields and flags added the same way. It still carries the old rule's `plate-dropped` flag, which
  `out/overlay.py` raises under any rule until R3 changes it. Replaced at the smoke step.
* **`create_version` still writes `modelFormat: 'built-model/1'`** for a v2 model (R5 changes it from the `stairways`
  key): the fixture store made by `tools/rooms/make-fixture-store.mjs` says `/1` until then.

## 6. Room ids and the label point

Ids are `<floor key>-R<nnn>`, three digits (`ground-R001`), numbered by rows from the top of the plan as R-3.1 says. The
tracer's label point is the room's centroid when it lies in the room, else shapely's representative point; R1 uses the
pole of inaccessibility. On the reference building both give the fixture's order.

## 7. The recorders are not tests

`test_r_old_rule.py` and `walk-parity.test.ts` define their recording test only when `WT_RECORD_OLD_RULE=1` /
`WT_RECORD_WALK_PARITY=1` is set, and the builder's `acceptance` tests are deselected without `WT_ACCEPTANCE=1`: a plain
run has no skipped test, as "Done when" asks.

## 8. `npm run seed` in "Done when" would move five files that R-13 pins

`npm run seed` is `... --data data --force`: it rewrites the five seeded versions of the owner's store. Their
`version.json` carries `seed.seededAt`, the moment of the seed, so each run changes those five files, which
`docs/rooms/reference/versions.sha1` pins ("every listed file is unchanged at the end", R-13) and which are tracked in
git (unchanged there since the baseline of 4 Nov: the upload milestone never ran the seed on `app/data` either). R-13's
own row for the seed is "a seed into a temp folder gives the committed `app/data` version files".

**R0 ran the seed into its temp folder, not on `app/data`**: five versions written with the five stamps
(`4b02dc17f286`, `c3a9408304a2`, `f1b95c53eb3d`, `e43270cc0c9b`, `6ab030750886`), 44 of the 49 listed files byte for
byte the store's, the five `version.json` equal but for `seed.seededAt`. The owner was using the app on 5710 / 8710 at
the time. RI: read "Done when"'s seed as the temp-folder seed, or make `seededAt` stable.
