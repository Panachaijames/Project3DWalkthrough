## 2. Items that need a decision

### D-1 The three v2 kinds live in `V2_KINDS`, beside `EXTRA_KINDS` (R-10 says "in `EXTRA_KINDS`")

`test_u4_maxscript.py:211` (frozen, must stay green) expects every kind of `EXTRA_KINDS` in its v1 test model
(`packages/viewer/test/fixtures/features.model.json`, which has none of the three). So they stay in the scaffold's
`V2_KINDS`, read beside `EXTRA_KINDS` through `OTHER_KINDS`: written the same way, only when the model uses them, after the
v1 extra kinds. Behaviour is R-10's. **Proposed:** R-10 reads "new kinds beside `EXTRA_KINDS` (`V2_KINDS`)".

### D-2 "A lost floor first" in the diff has no field of its own

`DiffBody` (upload-contracts.ts, `models_upload.py`, strict) has no place for it. So the builder orders `counts`: a floor
whose plate is 2 m2 or more smaller comes first and its plate row first; the web's `DiffPanel.tsx` (unchanged) lists the
rows in that order, so the lost floor is the first row of the list of changes. Two limits: JavaScript reorders object
keys that look like integers (a floor keyed `1`, `2` would not stay first), and the keys are text the panel shows as it
is (`rooms closed`, `plate area (m2)`, `stairs you can climb`). **Proposed:** R6 also sorts a `plate area (m2)` row with
`before - after >= 2` first itself, and shows the three keys with its own words.

### D-3 A new hole counts as a lost floor

The rule as written ("a floor's plate smaller by 2 m2 or more") is met by a hole the new rule cuts. Against their saved
old-rule versions the bank's Level 1 goes from 237.4 to 234.5 m2 (the lift shaft is cut now, 3.17 m2) and the house's
upper floor from 439.1 to 409.1 m2 (the double-height living room and the stair hall), so each is the first row of the
first new-rule rebuild's changes. Built as written. **Proposed, if that is noise:** compare the plate with its cut holes
added back (the model's `voids` areas), so only floor lost at the outline comes first; a staff "No floor here" (also a
hole) would then not come first either.

### Decided by the main session (11 Nov 2026, between batches)

* **D-1: accepted as built.** The kinds `tread`, `landing` and `ceiling` stay in `V2_KINDS`, read beside `EXTRA_KINDS`;
  RI writes "beside `EXTRA_KINDS`" into R-10.
* **D-2: accepted, with R6.** The builder keeps ordering `counts` with a lost floor first, and R6 sorts that row first
  in the panel itself and shows the three new keys (`rooms closed`, `plate area (m2)`, `stairs you can climb`) with
  their names, not as raw keys.
* **D-3: accepted as proposed, for RI.** A hole the new rule cuts on purpose (a stair well, a shaft, a marked void) is
  not "a lost floor": RI adds the cut holes back before the comparison in `out/diff.py` (one change, with a test on the
  bank's Level 1 lift shaft and the house's upper floor, which must then not come first).

## 3. How R3 read the design where it was silent or loose (information; accepted unless RI says otherwise)

* **N-1 The storey statement** is `setUserProp <PX>_nodes[<PX>_nodes.count] "<PX>_storey" <i>` on the line after the
  part call of every part with `stair` (treads, landings, wall pieces cut under a flight); `<i>` counts floors from 1, as
  `<PX>_floor`. A stair part whose stairway is not in the model, or rises to no floor of it, is a refusal
  (MaxScriptRefused), as an unknown floor is.
* **N-2 `__STOREYFIT__`** scales base and amount by `(new[up] - new[fi]) / (old[up] - old[fi])` only when both storeys
  are above 0; the stored base follows. A collapsed stair part (no Extrude) keeps its height and is counted with the
  parts that kept theirs (the window's existing sentence); the stair parts are not counted among "parts set to their
  level's wall height". The footer's message is the goldens' text, unchanged.
* **N-3 Hiding the Ceilings layers**: after the last part call, one `for` statement sets `isHidden` on each
  `<PX>_<floor>_Ceilings` layer that exists. The mock 3ds Max knows no layer visibility and no Extrude amount; the tests
  give it both for their own run (monkeypatched, put back after the test; the mock's file is not touched). Whether Max
  agrees is the 3D team's run, as for every line of the script.
* **N-4 The window** gets two labels under "Parts stay live ...": "Ceilings are on hidden layers <PX>_<level>_Ceilings:" /
  "switch them on for interior views."
* **N-5 `__STAIRTXT__`** is the stair sentence when the model has treads ("Stairs: 21 risers of 0.186 m, 19 treads and 1
  landing"; with several stairways each with its two floors, joined by "; "), else the hotel's sentence as before. The
  keep sentence leaves out treads and landings (they scale with their storey: the `stairs` note says so).
* **N-6 The top view** draws landings, then treads, right after the bank's steps (so walls are drawn over them, as over
  steps today); a model with treads or landings gets a title that names them, every other model the bank's title.
* **N-7 The Ceilings tick's label** is the viewer's default, "Ceilings" (`model/presentation.ts:58`): the presentation
  leaves labels to the viewer, as for every tick, so nothing is written for it.
* **N-8 The replay** adds "N rooms closed" (the sum of `facts.rooms[floor].spaces`) and "M stairs you can climb"
  (`facts.climbable`) at the end of the "Done:" list, after "rooms named". Ceilings are not mentioned (R-11 names the two).
* **N-9 A floor built with the new rule** is one whose report carries `slab` (a dict). Such a floor raises no
  `plate-dropped` of either kind. Its new flags come after every flag of before, floor by floor in the frozen fixture's
  order (slab-gap, room-open, room-names, doorway, stair, stair-clash, hole-skipped), then `stairs-none`. The `what` of
  each flag and its texts are the fixture maker's (`make-reference-building.py` `overlay_rooms`), so the ids are the
  fixtures'. The texts write "m2", as every flag of before (the design writes m²).
* **N-10 slab-gap**: a row of `slab.unfloored` of 4 m2 or more that is not answered; `ref {a, b}` and `at` the middle of
  the mouth, else `at` the row's own point and the sentence "Draw a line across the gap ...". The whole floor's flag is
  the old fallback with plate0: `slab.plate` under half of the convex hull of the floor's parts (not slabs, ceilings,
  treads, landings) and 100 m2 or more short of it.
* **N-11 room-open** of the second kind (`rooms.open` with `space`, R1.md N-12): "A closed area of N m2 with no door: is it
  floor?", `ref.room`. **room-names**: `at` is the middle of the room's bounding box when it lies in the room, else a point
  inside it.
* **N-12 stair-clash**: one flag per stair and floor; the rows (one per part and cut, R2.md N-19) counted by kind ("4 wall
  pieces and 1 lintel"); a row with `z1: null` (the piece taken out, `bank.apply_cuts`) is said apart: "... 1 lintel was
  taken out: no room under the stair." So the bank: "4 wall pieces of Level 1 under the stair were cut to the stair's
  underside. 1 lintel was taken out: no room under the stair." (its model holds 5 cut wall pieces: one cut left two).
* **N-13 hole-skipped** only for the reasons of R1.md N-17 that raise one: `the lowest floor` (info), `over 60 m2` and
  `over 30 % of the floor` (check, "An X marks N m2 as a void: is it one?", with the mark's box). The others are listed.
* **N-14 stairs-none** comes from the model's `stairways` and the floors' `stairways` rows (`state: 'climb'`, `to`), not
  from `X.new_rule['pairs']` (R2.md section 5): the pairs never reach the BuildResult (R0.md item 3). Raised only with
  `settings.options.stairs == 'climb'`; floors of one `stack` by level, a stair that passes a floor joins both pairs
  (R2.md N-16). The text lists the lower floor's stairs kept as lines (else the upper floor's), each with its text, else
  "No stair lines were found: is the stair's layer set to Stair on the Layers step?"; its floor and `at` are the first
  listed row's. On the five drawings no pair lacks a climbable stair, so none is raised.
* **N-15 fix-unmatched** for the new fixes: "The removed closing at (x, y) is no longer there ..." (`use: 'none'`);
  "The closing line at (x, y) is on a floor that is not in this build ..." (`use: 'line'`: R1 matches every line on a floor
  of the build); "The room fix at (x, y) finds no room ..."; "The stair fix at (x, y) finds no stair ...". `ref.fix` is the
  index in its own list; closings also carry `a`, `b`.
* **N-16 The diff's rows** are added only when a side is v2, and each only when a side has any; the plate area is the
  union of the floor's slab parts less their holes, to 0.1 m2. Two v1 models give the bytes they gave
  (`test_r_old_rule.py`).
* **N-17 The gap report** reads the build report from the version folder (`source/build-report.json`) or a run's out
  folder (`report.json`), else `--report` / `--proto-report`; its new rows are information rows (no gap row each), and
  only a storey outside its window and a probe with no floor are gap rows to classify.
