# Contract issues: U3, the model builder (upload milestone, 4 Nov 2026; finished 9 Nov 2026)

Written only by step U3 (docs/upload/WORK_BREAKDOWN.md U3). The integration step UI settles each item. "Information"
items say how U3 read a silent or loose spot of the design; "accepted" at integration means the design is read that way
from now on. Nothing here needs a frozen file changed for the bank: the port reproduces v2 byte for byte.

## 1. How the builder reports a failure (U1 to read; contract gap)

`contracts.build` has no error type, and U-3.4 / `RunErrorCode` need a code, a plain message and the step. U3 raises
`walkthrough_builder.build.errors.BuildError(code, message, step)` (also exported as `walkthrough_builder.build.BuildError`),
code one of `no_floors`, `bad_settings`, `area_empty`, `align_failed`, `detect_failed`, `build_failed`; `record()` gives
`JobRecord.error`'s `{code, message, step}`; `str(e)` is the message. Messages name floors and files by their names,
never a path. `MemoryError` and `RecursionError` are never wrapped (the step child exits 75, U-3.5). Any other exception
out of `build` is `build_failed`. **U1's `step.py` as written (9 Nov) already maps it** (it keeps any exception's `code`
attribute that is a RunErrorCode, and its `step`). UI: confirm, or move the class into `contracts.py`.

## 2. Grid labels whose `line` is only the bubble's leader (U2, UI)

U-9.4 gives a label to the floor whose area its `line` crosses. Both the scaffold's provisional reader
(`fixtures/upload/drawing/sukhumvit.drawing.json`) and U2's reader as it stood on 4 Nov (run into `%TEMP%\wt-u3\u2-reads`)
give the house's plan labels a 1.5 m leader stub (y 30.04 to 28.54 for a plan ending at y 25.85): no line crosses a
plan box, and auto alignment would fail. U3 keeps the rule and adds a bounded continuation (`build/align.py
assign_labels`): a line that crosses no area is continued away from its bubble for up to 10 m, and the label goes to the
FIRST area met along it (so the lettered bubbles at x 73.7 go to the upper plan, not to the ground plan behind them).
Elevation labels stay out (with no line, or lines running away from the plans). Result on both reads: the upper floor's
shift is (-74.0221, 0.0) m with a 0.9 mm spread (A-5: -74.021 +- 0.002), 16 shared labels in two families.
**ACCEPTANCE's "12 + 12 labels per floor"** cannot be met with either read: 27 of the 63 labels have no line at all, so
the ground gets 19 labels (11 numbered, 8 lettered) and the upper 16 (11, 5). That is the reader's (U2: U-7.3's "an end
within 1.0 m" finds the stub, not the grid line); the rule needs no change when the lines are longer. Re-run
`test_u3_align.py` and `test_u3_drawings.py::test_house_aligns_by_its_grid` with U2's final reads at integration.

## 3. Additions to the typed result (U4; information)

* `FixesReport.setAside` rows carry one more key, `key`: the cache key of the item (or INSERT) the fix matched, or null.
  **This answers U4-2** ("setAside entries carry no item keys"): the overlay's `setAside` should list it rather than the
  ref's own key, which differs when the fix matched by element id or sig (the source changed). One key per fix is
  enough: an INSERT's key covers its subtree by prefix, as the canvas reads it (U-12.1). The TypedDict types the row as
  `dict`, so the walker accepts it. U4's overlay still lists `fx['key']` (same result for key matches).
* `doorHeadsFrom` values: `'name' | 'floor' | 'drawing' | 'default'` as typed, plus **`'fix'`** for an opening fix's
  head (U-10.6). The model's opening `headFrom` keeps the prototype's strings (`'name'`, `'most common'`, `'default'`)
  and gains `'floor'` and `'fix'` (the bank has neither: its bytes are unchanged).
* `report.summary.namedHeads` has string keys (`"2.4"`, as JSON writes float keys), so the summary equals
  `bank_summary.json` in memory as well as on disk.
* `ReplayFacts.counts` = lines per category as the replay draws them (noise: the longest 6,000), equal to the scaffold
  fixture's; `ReplayFloor.sheet` is rounded to 2 decimals as the bank did (the rest of the replay is unrounded model
  coordinates, as U-10.8 says). Floors sharing a sheet sit at `[source position + sheetShift]`.
* `BuildResult.seconds`: `read`, `align`, `detect:<key>`, `walls`, `openings`, `furniture` (the JobRecord step names U1's
  worker gives the build child, so each step's seconds come from the build itself), `build` (walls to furniture) and
  `total`. The progress calls name the same steps: `align`, `detect:<key>`, `walls`, `openings`, `furniture`.
* Report positions (`singles[].a/b`, `railsLeftOut[].at`, `roomLabels[].at`, `platesDropped[].at`) are each floor's
  SHEET metres (its source's frame, before the alignment shift), rounded to 1 mm; `platesDropped[].at` is the plate's
  representative point. **`singles[].a/b`** are the ends of the longest straight run of the part's own line work that
  lies inside the part, so their middle (where U4 puts the single-line flag) is on the part; the part's long axis only
  when no line work is found. (The first version used the minimum rectangle's axis: on the tower's L-shaped and
  hooked single parts its middle lay up to 0.33 m off the part.) `swingsUnmatched[].at` is the swing's hinge (arc
  centre), sheet m. `railsLeftOut` lists the railings not built as thin bands and the stair rails off an upper floor
  (the bank's L2: 3), each with the keys of the railing lines it came from.
* `FloorUploadReport.itemsNoPoints` is the number of the floor's SOURCE items that have no points at all (they belong
  to no floor). U-9.1's "the house 2,267" is in fact the number of house items outside both plan areas (33,051 - 30,784
  in the reference cache); the house has 0 items without points, as have the other five drawings.
* `subtreesOutside`: INSERT subtrees with no majority in any floor that were left out whole, counted on the floor that
  held most of their points (the suite: 3).

## 4. Readings where the design is loose (information)

* **Whole-subtree reading (U-9.1)**: an INSERT goes with its subtree to the floor whose area (box + pad) holds MORE THAN
  HALF of its subtree's drawn points (insertion and text points when it draws nothing). One with no majority is walked
  into when it holds other INSERTs (a container such as a view block or an xref straddling two plans: its own INSERT
  item is then read per item), else left out whole and counted. Under `wholeFile` both readings are one list (the bank
  cannot move: tested). Line readers keep the per-item reading.
* **`furniture.pieceLayer: 'drawn'`**: "the majority of what is drawn in it is on furniture layers" is read as the suite
  prototype read it: the layer most of its drawn items are on (ties by name) has the furniture role; a block holding
  an INSERT on a furniture layer, or one that is itself such a candidate, is walked into.
* **`furniture.looseLayers`**: "layers whose loose lines become boxes (suite)" is read as the suite prototype read it:
  lines on those layers drawn outside every block (depth 0). With no `looseLayers` the bank's rule stands (lines on the
  furniture layers not inside a piece, at any depth: in a Revit export everything sits inside the view block).
* **`parapets: 'auto'`**: "only where the floor's wall layers carry solid fills" is read literally: the floor has at
  least one SOLID hatch (the cache item's `solid`) on a wall or column layer. Pattern hatches do not switch the rule on
  (the house's sheet has 9 `_USER` pattern hatches on its wall layer, none in its plans; the bank's 176 wall and column
  hatches, the suite's 26 and the tower's 409 are all SOLID). The rule itself still tests a piece against all the
  floor's wall fills, as the bank did. (The first version, 4 Nov, read "any hatch": the same result on the four
  drawings; changed 9 Nov to the design's word, tested on a synthetic plan with ANSI31 fills.)
* **Accepted outline edges (U-10.6 fix 3)**: walls 0.20 m on the side away from the room; glass 0.02 m (a pane), the
  same side. The thickness of an outline glass is not given by the design.
* **Opening fixes matched to a record whose kind is set to `none`**: the fix row's `matched` is the record's id (it did
  match); the record's `DetectorRecordOut.modelId` is null and its `fix` the fix's index.
* **Units**: the builder uses `settings.sources[i].units` (the staff's) and falls back to `SourceContext.units`; U1's
  `ensure` step makes the two agree.
* **Set-aside keys**: the matcher relies on U-7.2's key format (`/`-joined, an ATTRIB `<insert key>@<handle>`); an
  INSERT ref also removes the INSERT's attributes (`key@...`), not only `key/...`. The element INSERT of an item is the
  first `element_depth + 1` components of its key (scaffold note U0-6).
* **The matcher's sig, rsig and element id are the reader's** (as U2.md asks, 9 Nov): `build/fixes.py` calls
  `plangeom.ref_of(item, inserts_by_key, k)`, the function that fills the plan entries the browser copies into an
  ItemRef, so A-4's "every plan entry's sig / rsig / eid equal what the builder computes" holds by construction (tested
  against `plangeom.refs` on the synthetic plans, and the matcher's own copy of the rule gives the same values: it is
  used only when the reader's module cannot be imported). A signature that cannot be made (a non-finite coordinate) is
  '' and matches nothing, instead of failing the build.

## 5. Models every drawing can save (found 9 Nov by running each acceptance build through U4's outputs and the API's checks)

The API marks a done run `invalid_output` when its model fails `builtmodel.validate_bytes` (the frozen schema and the
D-5.6 rules), and U4's Max generator refuses a part it cannot extrude (`MaxScriptRefused` -> `build_failed`). The bank
passes both; three of the other drawings did not, each from a prototype habit the bank never triggered. The builder now
makes valid models (each change inert on the bank: its model is still `c3a9408304a2`; tests in `test_u3_rules.py`,
`test_u3_outputs.py`, `test_u3_drawings.py::test_outputs_pass_the_api_checks`):

* **A glass probe with floor on neither side is left out** (and logged): the schema's `probe.inside` has `minItems: 1`
  (the walk test walks at the glass from those sides) and the prototype wrote `inside: []` for glass standing away from
  every plate. The house had 5 (screens and facade glass outside the plates); a plan whose doors were not found has no
  plate inside its walls at all. Probe ids keep their numbers (gaps where one was left out).
* **A door with nothing above its head has no `lintelTop`** (absent instead of the prototype's `null`; the schema's
  `lintelTop` is a number or absent, and the viewer's parser refuses null too). The suite had 2; no bank door has one.
* **A part with a hole touching its outline, or two holes touching, is cut in two through that hole** (vertical cut,
  else horizontal, repeated until none is left; `build/model.py max_safe`): shapely calls such a polygon valid, but Max's
  Extrude cannot cap it and the generator refuses the whole model ("a hole touches the outline"). The tower had one wall
  network per floor (L29 and L30 `Wall_011`, 31 and 34 m2) whose 2.1 m2 hole touched its outline at one point; each
  is now two wall parts with the same footprint. Logged as "n part(s) added by cutting ...".
* **A window whose sill is at or over its head gets no pane** (a pane needs z1 > z0, D-5.6). Only mixed settings can
  cause it (a floor's sill with the project's head; the API checks each pair it sees together).

Measured after the changes (reference caches; `%TEMP%\wt-u3\scripts\e2e_outputs.py`, and the same with U2's 4 Nov reads
holding `k` keys, which give the same four models): bank `c3a9408304a2`, house `730ca65a45d3`, suite `205e9c2c46e8`
(`outlineWalls: 'suggest'`), tower `91414f408cb2`; all eleven outputs written and every `check_file` kind passing for
each. Flags: bank 20 (8 to check), house 64 (49), suite 73 (50), tower 98 (66).

## 6. Acceptance figures that are "should" and are not met exactly (information)

* **drawnVariant (U-11.7, should)**: all 26 pieces in the 9 families of `furniture_test/bank_furnished/analysis.md`
  come out `mirrorY` (front to back, as the analysis says), and two more pieces the analysis does not list: one of the
  three wall-hung basins (`Wall-hung vitreous china basin-Meridian-327242xx0`, L2-sanitary-003: 0.2 mm mirrored in y
  against 0.37 m as drawn, a real mirror) and one printer turned 180 degrees (a near-tie of a near-symmetric drawing).
  The test checks the 26 and allows these 2.
* **The suite's furniture against its prototype's 120 (should: within 5 %, every kind present)**: 127 (+5.8 %), every
  kind present; plants 12, seats 14, sofas 7, curtains 21, lamps 9, tall cabinets 3, bed, TV as the prototype. The
  differences come from the design's own rules: blocks holding chairs are walked into under `pieceLayer: 'drawn'` (the
  study's meeting table with three chairs and the banquette with two armchairs become their parts), pieces keep the
  layer of their INSERT when that is a furniture layer (`'drawn'` applies only to INSERTs on other layers), and 34
  loose-shape boxes against the prototype's 30 (its suite area was the wall cluster + 2 m, the settings' box is the
  detector's). The three blocks on `A-4. Dim` the prototype took as furniture are on a layer the settings call `other`.
* **The house's upper floor**: 32 pieces against the prototype's 29 (ground 47 = 47, kind for kind): the prototype's
  `settle()` (rug / zone outlines that enclose a smaller piece, the inner of two nested outlines) is not among
  U-10.5's rules and is not ported.
* **The house's three sliding glass doors** (`ground-004`, `-006`, `-018`, eitherKind) are glazed like windows (U-10.5,
  decision 27 Oct), so the self-check lists them as "something solid across them" and a walk through them stops at the
  pane. By design; A-6's door walks on the house should skip eitherKind doors or count them in GAPS.md (U7, UI).
